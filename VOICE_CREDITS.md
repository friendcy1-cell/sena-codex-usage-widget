# Japanese Widget Voice Credits

Japanese synthetic voices generated locally with Irodori-TTS by Chihiro Arata (Aratako), using the Irodori-TTS-v4.1-Anime fine-tune by phasefield-audio. Character voice references were originally generated with Irodori-TTS-v4-Small. These are synthetic fictional-character voices, not recordings of real voice actors.

- https://huggingface.co/phasefield-audio/Irodori-TTS-v4.1-Anime
- https://huggingface.co/Aratako/Irodori-TTS-v4.1-Small
- https://huggingface.co/Aratako/Irodori-TTS-v4-Small
- https://github.com/Aratako/Irodori-TTS
